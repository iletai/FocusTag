FocusTag turns physical friction into your superpower against doomscrolling.

Standard screen time limiters are broken: when dopamine cravings hit, your thumbs instinctively tap "Ignore Limit for 15 Minutes". FocusTag ends this cycle by requiring physical reality to unlock your distracting apps.

HOW IT WORKS:
1. Select your most addictive apps (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Games, Safari).
2. Pair any household NFC item: an elevator card, transit pass, or a $0.50 NFC sticker placed outside your bedroom or across the room.
3. When the shield is active, the ONLY way to unlock your apps is to stand up and tap that physical tag.

FEATURES:
- Unbreakable physical habit lock: Stop bypassing software limits.
- 100% Private & On-Device: Powered by Apple Screen Time & CoreNFC APIs. Zero server tracking, zero accounts needed.
- Emergency Shake Unlock: Away from your tag? Complete a 100-shake physical challenge for a brief 5-minute unlock.
- Battery-Friendly & Native: Crafted with pure SwiftUI for seamless iOS integration.
